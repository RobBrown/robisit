Meet Tab Mute Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Meet Tab Mute reads the titles and URLs of open Google Meet tabs on your device to display them in its popup. It stores nothing and sends nothing. It contains no analytics, no tracking, and no network code. It does not access meeting audio, video, chat, or participants.
